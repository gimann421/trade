# Round 24: dividend month premium, dividend growth, dividend payers (2026-10-20)

Standing order from Garret (2026-10-20): keep testing new methods round after round until one passes. Ideas chosen by the thread.
Hypotheses 173-175; 172 were tested before this round. Bar unchanged from rounds 6-23; nothing loosened.

**This section is written before any result of these tests was computed.**

## Data (new)
Alpaca's corporate-actions endpoint (free, same keys) returns cash dividends with ex-date and rate for every member ticker, including delisted names such as TWX, MON and RAI (checked). 20,068 dividends for 576 of the 719 member tickers, 2015-2026 (`research/round24_data.py`, `data/round24/dividends.csv`, git-ignored). There is no declaration date, so a dividend counts as known only from its ex-date (later than the market knew it; this is conservative for signals that depend on timing). A ticker that was renamed may show no dividends under its old name; that bias is the same for every name and small.

## Where the ideas come from
- **Dividend month premium** (Hartzmark-Solomon 2013, JFE, "The Dividend Month Premium"): stocks earn abnormal returns in the months in which they are expected to pay a dividend, because of price pressure from dividend-seeking demand; the premium is predicted by the stock having paid in that month in prior years. Later work finds it persists after publication. New to this repo and a branch off none of the earlier rules.
- **Dividend growth** (Michaely-Thaler-Womack 1995 on initiations and omissions; Chen et al. on dividend changes): firms that raise dividends signal confidence; the response is slow for large caps. Untested here.
- **Dividend payers versus non-payers**: a simple value-quality tilt; reported with a pass test because it costs nothing to add.

## Common setup
The round 18-23 monthly engine: decisions at month-end closes from 2017-01-31, fills at that close, held to the next decision, benchmark equal-weight priced point-in-time S&P 500 members, IS 2016-01-01..2022-12-31, OOS 2023-01-01 on, at least 48 / 36 rebalances, 5 bps (10 bps stress).
**Bar, required in both windows:** net active >= 2.0%/yr, information ratio >= 0.3, selection placebo >= 0.95 (1,000 random same-size baskets from the ranked names, seed 0), net active > 0 at 10 bps.
Regular dividends only (special = False). A dividend is known on its ex-date.

## Test 1 (hypothesis 173): `divmonth_predicted`
- At the month-end decision, the next calendar month is M. A name is a predicted payer if it had a regular ex-date in calendar month M twelve months earlier. Hold all predicted payers with a price, equal weight; at least 20 names, else the equal-weight of all priced members (no tilt that month).
- Ranked set for the placebo: all priced members. Mirror, report only: the names not predicted to pay.

## Test 2 (hypothesis 174): `divgrowth_quintile`
- For a name, the latest regular dividend on or before the decision day (ex-date within the last 135 days) compared with the regular dividend four payments earlier (ex-date 330 to 400 days before the latest one; at least five payments in the data); growth = latest rate / earlier rate - 1. Dividend history on or before the decision day only.
- Hold the 20% of scored names with the highest growth, equal weight (at least 20 scored names or the equal-weight of all priced members). Ranked set: scored names; benchmark: all priced members. Mirror, report only: the lowest-growth (cuts) quintile.

## Test 3 (hypothesis 175): `payers_tilt`
- Payers: at least three regular dividends with ex-dates in the 400 days up to the decision day. Hold all payers, equal weight. Ranked set: all priced members. Mirror, report only: non-payers.

## Multiple testing
Three rules, pre-specified, no variants; mirrors reported only. A pass would be a candidate for the shadow record only with Garret's say-so.

## Results (run 2026-10-20)

**Verdict: all three rejected (hypotheses 173-175).** Nothing is added to the shadow or paper records. Hypotheses tested after this round: 175.
`backtests/round24_tests/`. Coverage: predicted payers 145-163 of about 505 names in a typical month; dividend payers 376-404; growth scored for 72 names at the first decision (history starts in 2015) and 340-385 afterwards.

### Plain English
- **The dividend month premium** (stocks rise in the month they are expected to pay a dividend) is not there in large S&P 500 names: +0.6% a year before 2023 and negative after.
- **Dividend raisers** beat the average by about 1-2% a year, in both eras with the right sign, but not by enough to clear the 2% bar. It is the only mildly consistent sign this round; the placebo reached 0.95 only in 2023 on. The cutters did just as well since 2023, so I do not read it as a dividend signal.
- **Payers versus non-payers** made no difference.
