# Free data to retest the 10% residual momentum rule on 2000-2015 (2026-10-14)

**Short answer: no reliable free source exists for the stock rule itself.** The ETF rules were retested on clean data (round 7); the stock rule (H54, top decile of residual momentum in the S&P 500) was not, and cannot be without delisted-stock prices.

## What the rule needs
Point-in-time S&P 500 membership (have it: fja05680 file runs from 1996-01-02, 2,720 change rows) plus adjusted daily prices for every member including names later bought out or bankrupt.

## Checked, 2026-10-14
| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| Yahoo chart endpoint | Works for live tickers. 418 of the 865 tickers that were members at any month-end 2000-2015 return nothing (acquired, bankrupt, renamed). Lehman returns 404. Reused tickers can return a different company |
| Stooq | Not reachable from this environment |
| Tiingo, Nasdaq Data Link (Quandl WIKI) | Need a token / blocked (403) |
| Alpha Vantage | Adjusted daily is premium-only; free key is rate-limited |
| SEC DERA bulk files | 403 from here (EDGAR filings API itself works, as in round 8) |
| FMP, Twelve Data, Massive connectors | Sign-in expired; not tested. Free tiers of such vendors usually exclude delisted names and deep history |
| Ken French library | Works, survivorship-free (CRSP), but portfolios only: 49 industries, factors, momentum |

## Survivorship gap (`research/stock_coverage_2000_2015.py`)
Share of each month-end's S&P 500 members with Yahoo history from a year before to 25 days after that date, averaged by year:

| Year | 2001 | 2003 | 2005 | 2008 | 2010 | 2013 | 2015 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Usable | 48% | 52% | 53% | 60% | 65% | 70% | 67% |

Overall 56%. (2000 reads 0% only because the pull starts in 2000 and a year of history is required.) The missing 30-50% are the acquired and failed names, so a top-decile test on what remains would favour survivors, and momentum is especially exposed because losers drop out. The result would not be trustworthy in either direction.

## What can be done with free data
1. **Industry-level residual momentum on Ken French's 49 industries (1926-2026, delisting-free).** A different hypothesis (new ledger row, pre-registered), but a clean test of whether factor-stripped momentum earned a premium in 2000-2015 and of the November 2020-style factor reversals. Does not prove the stock rule.
2. **Use French's UMD and 10 momentum decile series as a sanity check** that momentum in general worked in 2000-2015 (it crashed in 2009), to say whether residual momentum had a regime to work in.
3. **Forward record** remains the only unseen data for the stock rule itself (decision 2026-10-30).
4. Paid options if the owner wants the real test: Sharadar (Nasdaq Data Link), Norgate, CRSP via a school account, or Tiingo's paid tier. Not purchased or tested.
