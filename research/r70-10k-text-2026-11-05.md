## Data and its limits (stated before the run)
Every original 10-K (form `10-K`) of a current member filed from 2015-10 on is downloaded from EDGAR (SEC_CONTACT in the User-Agent, never stored). The Item 7 (Management's Discussion and Analysis) text is cut out by pattern: the longest span between an "Item 7 ... Management's Discussion" heading and the next "Item 7A" or "Item 8 ... Financial Statements" heading (this skips table-of-contents lines). Tags are removed and tables removed first; if that leaves fewer than 1,000 words the filing is re-scored with table text kept. Filings with fewer than 1,000 words of MD&A are unscored (some filers place the MD&A in an exhibit or annual-report wrapper). Word list for tone: the Loughran-McDonald dictionary (Negative flag and syllable counts) as shipped in the pysentiment2 package (a 2020 edition of the list), kept in git-ignored data; words missing from it use a vowel-group syllable count. Parsing is imperfect and the coverage is part of the result.

## Rules (top 20% of scored names, equal weight, benchmark equal-weight scored names)
Filing used = the latest scored 10-K filed before the decision day with a report date within the 455 days before it.
- 290 `readable10k_quintile`: Gunning Fog index of the MD&A = 0.4 x (words per sentence + 100 x share of words with three or more syllables). Hold the lowest 20% (easiest to read).
- 291 `lowneg_tone_quintile`: share of MD&A words that are Loughran-McDonald negative. Hold the lowest 20%.
- Reported, not judged: mirrors (hardest to read / most negative); the correlation of 290 with 291; correlation with the three passes; tech/health share; and, for reference only, the change versus the prior year's value, which is not a rule.

## Common setup
Round 25-69 setup exactly: current S&P 500 members, monthly decisions from 2017-01-31, 5/10 bps, IS 2017-22, OOS 2023+, minimum 20 scored names. The first 10-Ks in the pull are from late 2015, so by 2017-01 coverage is incomplete for off-cycle fiscal years only.

## Bar, required in both windows
Net active >= 2.0%/yr, IR >= 0.3, placebo >= 0.95 (1000 draws, seed 0), net active > 0 at 10 bps. Correlation above 0.7 with an existing pass means the same bet. Both ideas are well published (Li 2008; Loughran-McDonald 2011) and must show they still work.

## Multiple testing
Two rules, no variants. Total tested after this round: 291.

## Results (run 2026-11-05)

**Verdict: both rejected (hypotheses 290-291).** Tests after this round: 291. `backtests/round70_tests/`. Reference rules reproduce (low accruals +2.8/+3.2, R&D +5.5/+15.8, sales/price +4.5/+3.3). 5,225 original 10-Ks of 501 members downloaded; 4,616 (88%) had at least 1,000 words of MD&A and were scored (85% for 2015-18 filings, 90% since); 270-449 names scored per month.

| Rule | IS 2017-22 | OOS 2023+ | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 290 easiest to read (lowest Fog) | -1.2% (IR -0.34, pl 0.12) | -1.3% (IR -0.46, pl 0.20) | rejected: wrong sign both windows |
| 291 least negative tone | -0.6% (IR -0.14, pl 0.25) | -0.1% (IR -0.03, pl 0.41) | rejected: nothing |

Reported, not judged: mirrors: hardest to read IS -1.6%, OOS +0.4%; most negative tone IS +1.4% (IR 0.42, pl 0.90), OOS +0.2%. Correlation of 290 with 291 -0.29. Correlation of 290 with low accruals 0.04, R&D -0.27, sales/price 0.14; 291: 0.07, 0.55, -0.65 (the tone rule is a mild tilt against value and toward R&D-heavy names, not a copy). Tech/health share 42% and 41%.

### Plain English
Companies whose annual reports are easiest to read did worse than average, not better, in both periods. Companies using the fewest negative words did nothing either. The old finding that plain-spoken or upbeat reports predict returns does not show up in the S&P 500 here. No pass.
